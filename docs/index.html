FAQ

Common BAi embroidery machine questions.

How much does a BAi embroidery machine cost?

Pricing can vary by model, delivery options, promotions, and financing structure. Use the form above to request current pricing or use the Buy Now buttons to view current online product pages.

Is financing available?

Yes. Financing is available through BYLD Finance with a soft credit pull only application process. All financing is subject to credit approval.

Which BAi machine is best for beginners?

THE MIRROR is usually the best fit for startups, home businesses, and buyers purchasing their first commercial embroidery machine.

What is the difference between THE MIRROR and THE VISION?

THE MIRROR is the compact single-head option with a 1,000 SPM speed and 500 x 350mm embroidery area. THE VISION steps up to 1,200 SPM and a 400 x 500mm embroidery area.

Can BAi machines embroider hats?

Yes. The models listed here include cap embroidery accessories such as cap frames, cap driver components, and a cap hooping station.

Who should consider THE VISION-2?

THE VISION-2 is best for shops that need more output, want to run duplicate jobs, or are ready to move into two-head production with one operator managing two heads.
